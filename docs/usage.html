Usage

Everything happens in your Telegram chat with your bot. It is button-first: you tap, you rarely type.

Commands

Open the menu button next to the message box to see these, or type them.

Command What it does
/list Show your rules, 10 per page. Tap a rule to enable, disable, edit, or delete it.
/add Add a rule: sender, then keywords, then confirm.
/interval Change how often Gmail is checked.
/status Show the interval, rule count, last check, and last error.
/help Show the command list.
/setai Open Settings for the optional keyword-idea helper (OpenRouter key and model).
/transfer Move the watcher to the Telegram account you are sending from.
/connect Replace the bot token after you generate a new one in BotFather.
/start <token> Bind your Telegram account. One-time, from the Setup link.
/cancel Abort the current step of a multi-step flow.

Adding a rule

A rule is one sender plus a set of keywords. /add asks for them one at a time.

  1. Send /add, then send the sender.
  2. Send the keywords, comma-separated.
  3. Confirm. The rule is created, enabled, and starts matching on the next check.

On the keywords step you may also see a [Use last keywords] button that copies the keywords from your most recent rule, and a [Need ideas?] button if the idea helper is set up.

Senders

  • A domain such as xxx.com matches any address at that domain.
  • A full email such as abc@xxx.com matches only that address.
  • A +tag is ignored, so mail to abc+sale@xxx.com matches abc@xxx.com.
  • Matching is case-insensitive.

Keywords

  • Comma-separated keyword means OR. air, 5折, sale matches any one of them.
  • Quotes match an exact phrase: "free shipping".
  • Case is ignored, and full-width and half-width characters are treated the same.
  • A keyword matches if it appears anywhere in the email body. The subject is shown, but matching uses the body.

Limits

  • Up to 30 enabled rules.
  • /list shows 10 rules per page.
  • Adding a sender that already has a rule opens that rule for editing instead of creating a duplicate.

Check interval

Use /interval and pick from the buttons. The options are 15, 30, and 60 minutes, then 2, 6, and 12 hours. The default is 15 minutes.

Status and errors

/status shows the interval, how many rules you have, when the last check ran, and the last error if there was one.

  • If a check finds nothing, you get no message.
  • If a send fails, the error is included in your next digest so you are not left guessing.
  • After three consecutive send failures, retries pause until a send succeeds again.

The digest

You get one message per check. Each email thread appears once, with the subject, the date, a link straight to the thread, and the keywords that matched.

  • There are no caps and no quiet hours. Mute your phone if you want a break.
  • Threads with nothing matched are not listed, and a check with no matches sends nothing.

Keyword ideas (optional)

Deal Watch can suggest keywords using OpenRouter, an AI provider. This is off until you set it up, and calls are billed to your own OpenRouter account.

  1. Send /setai to open Settings.
  2. Enter your OpenRouter API key and pick a model. The key is shown masked and can be deleted at any time.
  3. While adding or editing a rule, tap [Need ideas?], type a topic, and tap the keywords you want. They are handed back to the chat for you to confirm.

Suggestions only ever use the topic you type. They never read your email, and they never change your rules on their own.

Maintenance

  • New phone: nothing to do. Notifications follow your Telegram account.
  • New Telegram account: send /transfer from the new account and confirm.
  • New bot token: generate one in BotFather, then send /connect and paste it. The message is deleted right after.
  • Your rules and history survive all of the above, because they live in your Sheet, not in Telegram.
