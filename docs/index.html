Deal Watch

Get a Telegram message when a promotion email that matches your keywords lands in your Gmail inbox.

Get started See how to use it

What it does

  • Watch chosen senders

    A whole domain such as xxx.com, or a single address such as abc@xxx.com.

  • Match your keywords

    Comma-separated keywords work as OR. Put a phrase in quotes to match it exactly.

  • One digest per check

    Matches are grouped per email thread with the subject, date, and a link straight to the thread.

  • Button-first Telegram

    Add rules, change the check interval, and read status by tapping buttons. Typing is only a fallback.

How it works

  1. You copy a Google Sheet template and set it up in your own Google account.
  2. Your Gmail is checked on a schedule (15 minutes to 12 hours) for emails from your senders that contain your keywords.
  3. Matches are collapsed into a single digest, grouped per thread.
  4. Deal Watch sends that digest to your Telegram chat. Nothing found means no message.
No server, no database, no third-party service

Deal Watch runs inside your own Google account as an Apps Script bound to your own Sheet. Your bot token and optional OpenRouter key stay in your account and are never sent anywhere else.

What you need

  • A Google account with Gmail.
  • A Telegram account.
  • A bot created in Telegram's BotFather (a few taps — the setup guide walks you through it).
  • About 15 minutes.
  • Optional: an OpenRouter account and API key, only if you want the AI keyword-idea helper.
